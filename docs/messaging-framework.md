# Messaging framework

## Primary audience

Footnote is for people whose best thinking happens somewhere else — on a run, between meetings, on a long walk home. They're readers, writers, builders, people who fill notebooks and forget where they put them. What makes them different: they already have ideas. They just lose them. They've tried voice memos (too disorganized), Notes (too much friction), and journaling (too stationary). They want capture that keeps up with them.

## Core insight

Movement loosens the mind. Walking creates a specific cognitive state — not quite focused, not quite idle — where connections form that won't form at a desk. But the tools we have for capturing ideas were designed for sitting still. Footnote is built on the premise that thinking on foot is a distinct activity that deserves its own instrument.

## Positioning statement

Footnote is the journal that walks with you — capturing where you were, when you thought it, and exactly what you meant to say.

## Message hierarchy

1. Your best ideas happen while you're moving. Footnote is how you keep them.
2. Every note is stamped with the time and place you thought it — context you'll want later.
3. Nothing to sign up for. Nothing stored on a server. Just you, your walk, and your words.
4. Designed for thumbs in motion: one screen, one job, zero friction.
5. Your data stays on your phone, always. Footnote has no interest in it.
6. Export your walks your way — plain text now, richer formats with Pro.
7. Built to last offline: no signal, no problem.

## Tagline candidates

1. **Walk. Think. Keep it.** *(minimal, rhythmic — works on a splash screen)*
2. **The journal that knows where you were.** *(direct, slightly mysterious)*
3. **Thoughts move. So does Footnote.** *(witty, movement double-meaning)*
4. **Every great idea has a location.** *(conceptual, poetic)*
5. **Stop losing the walk-home thoughts.** *(direct, calls out the pain point)*

## Anti-vocabulary

| Word / phrase | Why to avoid |
|---|---|
| "seamless" | Meaningless filler; every app claims this |
| "productivity" | Wrong frame — this is about thinking and reflection, not output |
| "capture your thoughts" (generic) | Too weak to lead with |
| "on the go" | Positions Footnote as an afterthought rather than primary tool |
| "AI-powered" | Lead with the human outcome (clean, usable transcripts) not the mechanism |
| "mindfulness" / "wellness" | Wrong framing; misrepresents who it's for |
| "sync across devices" | Not a feature yet; don't promise what isn't there |
| "simple" (alone) | Vague; show simplicity through description ("one screen, one job") |

## Voice notes

- **Unhurried** — Nothing urgent, no exclamation points, no pressure
- **Precise** — Short sentences, no waste, every word earns its place
- **Warm** — Not clinical, not startup-bro. Closer to an independent bookshop than a productivity SaaS
- **Honest** — No hype, no "revolutionary." State facts. Let the product make the case.
