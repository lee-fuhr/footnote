## What's coming — features in voting

**Echo**
After a walk, Echo reads back your notes and writes you a short letter — in second person, from the walk's perspective. Not a summary. Something closer to a reflection. The kind of thing you'd want to find in a journal three years from now.

**Velocity**
Your writing speed and your walking pace, mapped against each other and laid over your route. It turns out when you write fast, you were somewhere. Velocity shows you where.

**Morning Brief**
At 7am, a three-line distillation of yesterday's walk. No app to open. Just a quiet notification that says: here's what you were thinking about.

Vote in-app to push the ones you care about up the list.
