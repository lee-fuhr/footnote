# Help documentation

## Getting started — your first walk

Open Footnote and tap **Start your walk**. That's it. You don't need an account, you don't need to set anything up. The app opens to a blank page, ready for your first thought. When you're done, tap **End walk** — Footnote saves everything automatically and adds the session to your walk history.

## How notes work — what gets captured automatically

Every line you type gets two things attached to it: the exact time you wrote it, and your GPS location at that moment. You don't have to do anything to make this happen — it's automatic. When you look back at a walk later, you'll see your notes in sequence with timestamps, and a map showing where each thought landed. If you typed something at the park and something else at the corner two blocks over, Footnote knows the difference.

## Offline mode — using Footnote without internet

Footnote works completely offline. If you start a walk without a signal — underground, in the hills, somewhere remote — the app keeps working. Your notes save to your phone's storage directly. GPS still works without internet on most phones (it uses satellites, not cell towers). The only thing that needs a connection is the AI Pack's voice transcription feature; everything else is fully local.

## Your walk history — finding past sessions

Every walk you've completed is saved in your session list. Each session shows the date, approximate duration, and the first line of your notes as a preview. Tap any session to read the full walk. Pro users get a richer view with route details and more context per session.

## Exporting your notes

You can export any walk as a plain text file (.txt) from the session view — available on the free tier. Pro users can also export as Markdown (.md), which works well with Obsidian, Notion, or any Markdown-aware text editor. To export: open a session, tap the share icon, choose your format.

## Tiers and pricing

Footnote has three tiers. **Free** is free forever — unlimited walks, location and time capture, .txt export, everything you need. **Pro** is a one-time purchase ($4.99 at launch, $7.99 regular) with a three-month free trial; it adds .md export and richer session history, and once you buy it, it's yours with no ongoing charge. **AI Pack** is a subscription (~$3/month, currently waitlisted) that adds voice transcription and AI cleanup for spoken notes.

## Privacy and your data

Your notes never leave your phone unless you export them yourself. Footnote has no server, no account, no cloud sync. We don't collect your location data, your notes, or anything else. The GPS pins are stored locally alongside your text. We have no access to any of it. If you delete the app, the data goes with it — there's no backup on our end, so export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

## Installing on your phone (PWA)

Footnote is a Progressive Web App — it installs from your browser, no app store required.

**On iPhone:** Open Footnote in Safari, tap the Share button (the box with an arrow), and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It'll appear on your home screen like any other app and work offline.

**On Android:** Open Footnote in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, and choose **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**. Once installed, it behaves like a native app.

## Using voice input (Siri / Google Assistant)

Footnote doesn't have its own built-in voice input yet — that's the AI Pack, which is on the waitlist. In the meantime, use your phone's system keyboard: on iPhone, tap the microphone icon on the keyboard to dictate into any note field. On Android, the same icon appears on Gboard. You'll want to review what it transcribed, but it works for capturing the gist of a thought while your hands are full.
